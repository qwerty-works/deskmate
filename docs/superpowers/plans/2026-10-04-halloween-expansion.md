Goal: add exactly five unique animations, selected and orchestrated by the agent, preserving the original six.

- Bone Boogie: rubber-hose skeleton dancing and bowing; witch/skeleton agent.
- Knock Knock: cinematic haunted doorway reveal and slam; door/vortex/hand agent.
